4. Roadmap

This browser app supports separate Cash and GPP rankings, salary and roster validation, CSV input, heuristic simulation, diversification, and CSV export. V5 adds an optional current-season PPR matchup estimation model (only when current-year source data is available). Next: injury/status feeds, matchup-based projections, calibrated ownership, opponent lineup simulations, actual contest result tracking, and backtesting. None of those advanced components are claimed to exist yet.
